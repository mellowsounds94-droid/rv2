About Us

Learning to drive, done right.

Right Vehicle To Learners Driving School has helped hundreds of learners across Austral and the surrounding suburbs become safe, confident drivers. Our instructor is known for a calm, patient teaching style that meets you exactly where you are — whether that's your very first time behind the wheel or a final polish before your test.

We focus on the fundamentals that actually matter for the test and for real life: lane changes, head checks, parking, and staying calm under pressure. Many of our students pass on their first attempt.

  • Patient, structured lessons tailored to your level
  • Dual-control vehicle for a safe learning environment
  • Support converting overseas licences to NSW licences
  • Flexible scheduling, 7 days a week

Meet Your Instructor

Vijay

Vijay is a patient, experienced driving instructor with over 10 years behind the wheel and 3+ years teaching new drivers across Western Sydney. He specialises in calm, structured lessons that build genuine confidence — not just enough to scrape through a test.

Every lesson is tailored to where you're actually at, whether that's your very first time behind the wheel or fine-tuning before test day. Clear communication and steady, constructive feedback are central to how he teaches, and many students pass on their first attempt.

  • 10+ years driving, 3+ years as an instructor
  • Fluent in English, Nepali, and conversational Hindi
  • Dual-control vehicle for a safe learning environment

What We Offer

Lessons Built Around You

Tap a course to see who it's for, what's covered, and how long it typically takes.

🧭

Beginner Lessons

Never driven before? Start from the basics — controls, road rules, and building confidence at your own pace.

Learn more ▾

Who it's for: First-time drivers with little or no experience behind the wheel.

What's covered: Vehicle controls, mirrors and seating setup, moving off and stopping smoothly, steering control, and basic road rules.

Typical duration: 1–2 hour lessons; most learners start with a block of 5–10 hours.

🛣️

Standard Driving Lessons

Structured lessons covering lane changes, head checks, parking, roundabouts, and defensive driving.

Learn more ▾

Who it's for: Learners with some experience who want structured, ongoing skill-building.

What's covered: Lane changes, head checks, roundabouts, parallel and reverse parking, give-way rules, and defensive driving.

Typical duration: 1–2 hour lessons, booked as an ongoing series as your skills progress.

✅

Test Day Support

Final preparation and, where available, use of our dual-control vehicle on the day of your driving test.

Learn more ▾

Who it's for: Learners with an upcoming driving test who want a final confidence check.

What's covered: Mock test run-through, common test routes, last-minute tips for nerves, and pick-up near the test centre.

Typical duration: One 1–2 hour lesson directly before your test, plus use of our dual-control vehicle on the day (where available).

🌏

Overseas Licence Conversion

Guidance and practice sessions to help you convert your overseas licence to a valid NSW driving licence.

Learn more ▾

Who it's for: Licensed drivers who've recently relocated and need to convert to a NSW licence.

What's covered: NSW road rules and signage, local driving conventions, test-specific requirements, and guidance on the paperwork involved.

Typical duration: 2–5 hours, depending on your existing driving experience.

💪

Confidence Building

Nervous or anxious behind the wheel? Calm, encouraging coaching designed to build real confidence.

Learn more ▾

Who it's for: Nervous or anxious drivers, including learners who've had a difficult experience before.

What's covered: A calm, unhurried pace, quiet-road practice before busier roads, and patient repetition of the skills that feel hardest.

Typical duration: Flexible — sessions are paced entirely around your comfort level.

🔄

Refresher Lessons

Been off the road a while? Get back up to speed with a tailored refresher package.

Learn more ▾

Who it's for: Licensed drivers who haven't driven in a while and want to rebuild confidence.

What's covered: A quick reassessment of your skills, updated road rules, motorway driving, and parking practice.

Typical duration: 1–2 hour sessions, usually 2–4 lessons in total.
