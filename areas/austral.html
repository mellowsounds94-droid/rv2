Your Local Austral Driving School

Right Vehicle To Learners is based at 77 Narrami Rd, right in Austral — so if you're learning to drive in Austral, your lessons start close to home rather than eating into your lesson time with a long pickup drive. Vijay, our RMS-qualified instructor, has spent 10+ years driving and 3+ years teaching new drivers across Austral and the surrounding Western Sydney suburbs, in a dual-control automatic vehicle with air conditioning and power steering.

Learning to Drive on Austral's Roads

Austral is still a developing area, which means a genuinely useful mix for learners: quiet residential streets to build the fundamentals, alongside growing traffic and key arterial roads like Bringelly Road and Fifteenth Avenue to practise the observation and decision-making skills your test will actually assess. Lessons are structured to move you from the quiet streets into busier conditions at a pace that matches where you're at — not a fixed schedule.

Your Nearest Driving Test Centres

Austral doesn't have its own Service NSW centre, but two options are a short drive away: the Liverpool Service Centre (Liverpool Megacentre, 2/20 Orange Grove Rd, Liverpool) and the newer Edmondson Park Service Centre (Level 1/52 Soldiers Parade, inside Ed.Square), which also offers practical driving tests by appointment. Test Day Support lessons can include a warm-up drive around your chosen test centre's local roads before your booking.
