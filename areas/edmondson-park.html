Driving Lessons for Edmondson Park Learners

Right Vehicle To Learners is based just next door in Austral, so Edmondson Park is one of our core service areas. Vijay, our RMS-qualified instructor, has 10+ years driving and 3+ years teaching experience, and lessons are built around exactly where you're at — from complete beginners to learners polishing test-specific skills before booking.

Your Edmondson Park Test Centre Advantage

Edmondson Park has its own Service NSW centre at Level 1/52 Soldiers Parade, inside Ed.Square — and it offers practical driving tests by appointment, not just knowledge tests and licence applications. That's a genuine advantage for local learners: your Test Day Support lesson can include a warm-up drive on the actual roads around your test centre, without a long trip across Sydney first.

Learning to Drive Around Edmondson Park

Edmondson Park mixes newer residential streets with busier connector roads and ongoing development traffic — a genuinely useful environment for building the observation and hazard-awareness skills your test will assess. Lessons progress from quieter streets into these busier conditions at your pace, not a fixed schedule.
