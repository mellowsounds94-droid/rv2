Driving Lessons for St Marys Learners

Right Vehicle To Learners serves St Marys as one of our core areas alongside our home base in Austral. Vijay, our RMS-qualified instructor, has 10+ years driving and 3+ years teaching experience, and every lesson is one-on-one and paced around exactly where you're at — from your very first time behind the wheel to a final polish before test day.

Your St Marys Test Centre Advantage

St Marys has its own dedicated driver testing centre at 75-105 Mamre Road, St Marys NSW 2760, offering both computer tests (the Driver Knowledge Test and Hazard Perception Test) and the practical driving test on-site. That means your Test Day Support lesson can include a genuine warm-up drive on the local roads around the centre, not just a general lesson somewhere else in Sydney.

Learning to Drive Around St Marys

St Marys combines established residential streets with busier main roads and nearby motorway access — useful conditions for building confidence with lane changes, give-way rules, and the kind of traffic you'll actually encounter on test day. Lessons build up from quieter streets into these conditions at your own pace.

Quick Answers

Where is the St Marys driver testing centre?

75-105 Mamre Road, St Marys NSW 2760. It offers the Driver Knowledge Test, Hazard Perception Test, and practical driving test on-site, with on-site parking entering from Hall Street.

Do you offer pickup and drop-off in St Marys?

Yes — just provide your address when booking and we'll pick you up and drop you off at the end of the lesson.
